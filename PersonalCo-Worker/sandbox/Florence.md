# Best Italian Restaurants in Florence

## 1. Osteria Lucignolo
- **Rating:** 5.0/5 (647 reviews)
- **Highlights:** 
  - Excellent food presentation with diverse menu options, both classic and innovative.
  - Known for a wonderful dining atmosphere and complimentary bubbles.
  - Highly rated for food quality, service, and atmosphere.
- **Typical Price:** €30 and under
- **Menu Highlights:** Traditional Tuscan dishes, unique pasta, and possibly seasonal specials.

## 2. Bambi Trippa E Lampredotto
- **Rating:** 4.7/5 (77 reviews)
- **Highlights:** 
  - Famous for its authentic street food experience with amazing meat sandwiches, particularly lampredotto.
  - Recognized for its quality raw materials and rich flavors.
- **Typical Price:** €1-10
- **Menu Highlights:** Lampredotto sandwiches, tripe dishes, and local specialties.

## 3. Osteria Cancello Dei Macci
- **Rating:** 9.5/10 (TheFork)
- **Highlights:** 
  - Exceptional quality of food with great hospitality and a warm atmosphere.
  - Dishes made from fresh ingredients; they even grow some themselves.
- **Typical Price:** €30 and under
- **Menu Highlights:** Burata & tomatoes appetizer, mezzeluna truffle ravioli, osso buco.

---

These restaurants offer a delightful taste of Italian cuisine with warm atmospheres and reasonable prices. For more details, it's recommended to check their menus directly.
