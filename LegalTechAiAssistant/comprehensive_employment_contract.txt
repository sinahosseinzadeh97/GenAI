COMPREHENSIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on January 15, 2024, between TechCorp International Ltd., a corporation organized under the laws of Delaware ("Company"), and Sarah Johnson, an individual ("Employee").

ARTICLE 1: EMPLOYMENT AND DUTIES

1.1 Position and Responsibilities
The Company hereby employs Employee as Senior Software Engineer, reporting directly to the Chief Technology Officer. Employee shall perform all duties and responsibilities typically associated with this position, including but not limited to:
- Design, develop, and maintain software applications
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams on product development
- Participate in code reviews and technical documentation
- Mentor junior developers and contribute to team knowledge sharing
- Ensure compliance with software development best practices and security protocols

1.2 Full-Time Employment
This is a full-time position requiring Employee's exclusive professional services during business hours. Employee agrees to devote their best efforts, skills, and attention to the performance of duties under this Agreement.

1.3 Location and Remote Work
Employee's primary work location shall be the Company's headquarters at 1234 Technology Drive, San Francisco, CA 94105. The Company may, at its discretion, allow remote work arrangements subject to operational requirements and performance standards.

ARTICLE 2: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

2.1 Base Salary
Employee shall receive an annual base salary of $145,000, payable in accordance with the Company's standard payroll practices. Salary reviews will be conducted annually, with any adjustments at the Company's sole discretion based on performance and market conditions.

2.2 Performance Bonus
Employee may be eligible for an annual performance bonus of up to 20% of base salary, contingent upon achievement of individual and company performance metrics as determined by the Company's compensation committee.

2.3 Equity Compensation
Subject to approval by the Board of Directors, Employee will be granted stock options to purchase 5,000 shares of Company common stock at the fair market value on the grant date, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff.

2.4 Benefits Package
Employee shall be entitled to participate in all employee benefit programs maintained by the Company, including:
- Health, dental, and vision insurance (Company pays 80% of premiums)
- 401(k) retirement plan with 4% company matching
- Life and disability insurance coverage
- Flexible spending accounts for healthcare and dependent care
- Employee assistance program and wellness initiatives

2.5 Paid Time Off
Employee shall accrue paid vacation at the rate of 20 days per year, with the ability to carry over up to 5 unused days to the following year. Additionally, Employee is entitled to 10 sick days annually and all Company-recognized holidays.

ARTICLE 3: CONFIDENTIALITY AND PROPRIETARY INFORMATION

3.1 Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" includes all non-public, proprietary, or confidential information disclosed by the Company, including but not limited to:
- Technical data, trade secrets, know-how, research, product plans
- Customer lists, supplier information, pricing strategies
- Financial information, business plans, marketing strategies
- Software code, algorithms, system designs, and technical specifications
- Personnel information and internal business processes

3.2 Confidentiality Obligations
Employee agrees to:
- Maintain strict confidentiality of all Confidential Information
- Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
- Use Confidential Information solely for the performance of duties under this Agreement
- Take reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized disclosure
- Return all Confidential Information upon termination of employment

3.3 Survival of Obligations
The confidentiality obligations set forth in this Article shall survive termination of this Agreement and continue indefinitely, or until such information becomes publicly available through no breach by Employee.

ARTICLE 4: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 Work Product Ownership
All inventions, discoveries, improvements, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Employee during the course of employment shall be the exclusive property of the Company. This includes all work performed using Company resources or relating to Company business.

4.2 Assignment of Rights
Employee hereby assigns to the Company all rights, title, and interest in and to any Work Product, including all patent, copyright, trademark, and trade secret rights. Employee agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect the Company's ownership rights.

4.3 Pre-Existing Intellectual Property
Employee represents that they have disclosed all pre-existing intellectual property that might be relevant to their employment. Any such pre-existing IP shall remain the property of Employee, provided it does not conflict with Company interests.

4.4 Moral Rights Waiver
To the extent permitted by law, Employee waives all moral rights in Work Product and agrees not to assert such rights against the Company or its successors.

ARTICLE 5: NON-COMPETITION AND NON-SOLICITATION

5.1 Non-Competition Covenant
During employment and for a period of 12 months following termination, Employee agrees not to directly or indirectly engage in any business that competes with the Company's core business activities within the geographic markets where the Company operates.

5.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees
For 18 months following termination, Employee shall not solicit, recruit, or induce any Company employee to leave their employment or join a competing business.

5.3 Non-Solicitation of Customers
For 12 months following termination, Employee shall not solicit or attempt to divert any Company customers or clients with whom Employee had material contact during the 24 months preceding termination.

5.4 Reasonableness and Enforceability
The parties acknowledge that the restrictions in this Article are reasonable in scope, duration, and geographic area, and are necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests.

ARTICLE 6: TERMINATION

6.1 Termination by Company for Cause
The Company may terminate Employee's employment immediately for cause, including but not limited to:
- Material breach of this Agreement or Company policies
- Conviction of a felony or crime involving moral turpitude
- Gross negligence or willful misconduct in the performance of duties
- Unauthorized disclosure of Confidential Information
- Violation of securities laws or regulations

6.2 Termination by Company without Cause
The Company may terminate Employee's employment at any time without cause upon 30 days' written notice. In such case, Employee shall receive severance pay equal to 8 weeks of base salary, subject to execution of a separation agreement.

6.3 Termination by Employee
Employee may terminate employment at any time upon 2 weeks' written notice to the Company. No severance benefits shall be payable in case of voluntary resignation.

6.4 Death or Disability
This Agreement shall terminate automatically upon Employee's death. In case of permanent disability preventing performance of essential job functions, the Company may terminate employment after reasonable accommodation efforts.

ARTICLE 7: POST-EMPLOYMENT OBLIGATIONS

7.1 Return of Company Property
Upon termination, Employee shall immediately return all Company property, including but not limited to:
- Laptop computers, mobile devices, and other equipment
- Company credit cards, access badges, and keys
- All documents, files, and materials containing Confidential Information
- Any other property belonging to the Company

7.2 Cooperation in Legal Matters
Employee agrees to cooperate with the Company in any litigation, investigation, or regulatory proceeding in which Employee's knowledge or testimony may be relevant, subject to reasonable compensation for time and expenses.

ARTICLE 8: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Mandatory Arbitration
Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Employment Arbitration Rules.

8.2 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict of law principles.

8.3 Injunctive Relief
Notwithstanding the arbitration provision, either party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to enforce the confidentiality, non-competition, or intellectual property provisions of this Agreement.

ARTICLE 9: GENERAL PROVISIONS

9.1 Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements relating to the subject matter hereof.

9.2 Amendment
This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by both parties.

9.3 Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

9.4 Assignment
The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor or affiliate. Employee may not assign this Agreement without the Company's prior written consent.

9.5 Notices
All notices required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by certified mail, email, or personal delivery to the addresses set forth below.
